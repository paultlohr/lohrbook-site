Lohrbook LiDAR: privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026. This covers the Lohrbook LiDAR iPhone app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't receive your scans, photos, measurements or any other information about you or your device.

What stays on your device

Everything the app records is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage: 3D scans of rooms, the measurements derived from them, and the photos the app takes while you scan together with the position of the camera for each photo. The photos are not added to your Photos library. Your data leaves the phone only when you choose to send it, for example with Share, AirDrop, email, or by copying files out through the Files app. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup under your own backup settings.

Camera and LiDAR

The app uses the camera and the LiDAR sensor to measure the space you point it at and to take photos for the scan. This is processed on your iPhone by Apple's ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks. Nothing is sent to us. Scans can show whatever was in view, including people and personal belongings, so be mindful when you share them.

Purchases

The app is free to scan with and to export the standard 3D and CAD file formats. An optional one-time "Full data" purchase unlocks the raw scan data. The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes what is collected, this page will say so before it happens.
