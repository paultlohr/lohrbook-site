Install the beta

Mac users, check this first: Scan Office needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. It does not run on Intel Macs. To check yours: Apple menu › About This Mac. If it says "Chip: Apple M…", you're set. If it says "Processor: … Intel", Scan Office won't open on it. The iPhone app works either way.

Scan Office runs on the office Mac. Scan Sheet runs on each crew member's iPhone. Both are free in the beta, until March 2, 2027. Some features may become part of a paid subscription later.

Joining: beta spots are limited, so testers are added by hand. Email paultlohr@gmail.com. You'll get an iPhone invite from Apple's TestFlight and a reply with the Mac download.

Scan Office (Mac)

You need: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Not Intel Macs. If you'd use it on an Intel Mac, email and say so; requests are counted.

  1. Download Scan Office 1.35.0 beta.dmg from the link in your welcome email.
  2. Open the .dmg. Drag Scan Office onto the Applications folder in that window.
  3. Open Scan Office from Applications.
  4. If your Mac asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet, click Open. The app is signed and checked by Apple.
  5. If it asks to let Scan Office find devices on your local network, click Allow. Without it, phones can't find the office by themselves.

It opens on Today with no job open. Next: the Scan Office guide.

Scan Sheet (iPhone)

  1. Open the TestFlight invite email on your iPhone (check spam if it isn't there).
  2. It installs Apple's free TestFlight app, then Scan Sheet.
  3. The first time you scan, allow the camera. The first time you link to an office, allow the local network.

Next: the crew guide.
